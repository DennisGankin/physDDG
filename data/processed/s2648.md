# s2648 (Dehouck et al. 2009, Bioinformatics 25:2537, the PoPMuSiC training set)

**What it is.** 2,648 single mutations on 131 PDB entries (132 entry×chain), with pH and temperature. This is the classic larger curated low-throughput set derived from ProTherm.

**File source.** `prostata/S2648.csv` from github.com/mitiau/PROSTATA, with columns PDB_CHAIN, WILD_RES, POSITION, MUTANT_RES, PH, TEMPERATURE and EXP_DDG. It was chosen over Q3421 (ThermoNet), whose values carry known sign inconsistencies. For example, 1A23 H32L appears as +4.95 in Q3421 but −4.6 (destabilising) in S669/KORPM.

**Sign.** The file has negative = destabilising (mean −1.01), so it was flipped. After the flip, buried hydrophobic → small/polar has mean +2.48, 97% > 0.

**Mapping.**
- Exact author numbering with the WT letter required to match: 2,633/2,648 rows.
- **15 rows dropped**: all of 1LVE (an immunoglobulin light chain with Kabat numbering and insertion codes). Its positions are sequential indices. Per the PI's rule there are no offset rescues; running `13_build_external.py --allow-offset` would recover them with a sequential index.
- 3.9% of rows are near a HETATM; 5.2% are near another chain.

**Homology.**
- 16/132 chains (6.4% of rows) have a Megascale-train homologue.
- **65/132 chains (68.6% of rows) have a FireProt-trainval homologue.** S2648 and FireProt both derive from ProTherm, so S2648 is best used as an extra fitting or CV set, not as an independent test.

ddg: mean 1.02, sd 1.47, 45.1% > 1. Extra columns: pH, temp.
