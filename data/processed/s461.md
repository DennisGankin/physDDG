# s461 (Martin-Hernandez et al. 2023, Bioinformatics 39:btad011, the KORPM paper)

**What it is.** A curated subset of S669: 461 mutations on 48 proteins. The authors corrected S669 errors and removed mutations likely to be functional (e.g. at oligomer interfaces).

**File source.** `korpm/S461.txt` (github.com/chaconlab/korpm), the authors' own repository. Columns are PDB, WT·chain·resnum·mut and ddG. The 4th column is always 0.000 and is ignored. There is no pH or temperature.

**Sign.** In the file, ddG < 0 = destabilising (mean −1.14), so it was flipped. After the flip, buried hydrophobic → small/polar has mean +2.36, 99% > 0.

**Overlap with S669.** All 461 rows are present in s669.csv with the same PDB, chain, resnum and mutation.

**Structures and mapping.** Same procedure as s669: local mirror, exact numbering, 461/461 mapped, 0 dropped. 7.4% of rows are near a HETATM and 5.9% near another chain in the asymmetric unit; the latter are probably crystal contacts, since the authors removed interface mutations.

**Homology.**
- 5/48 proteins (37.1% of rows) have a Megascale-train homologue, all at ≥50% identity.
- 2/48 proteins have a FireProt-trainval homologue.

ddg: mean 1.15, sd 1.25, 47.9% > 1.
