# s669 (Pancotti et al. 2022, Brief. Bioinform. 23:bbab555)

**What it is.** 669 direct single mutations on 94 protein chains. The set was built to have less than 25% identity to S2648 and VariBench. The sign follows Pancotti et al.

**File source.** `korpm/S461_results/S669.txt` from github.com/chaconlab/korpm (redistributed by the S461 authors), downloaded by `scripts/10`. The original S669 supplementary was not fetched. This copy has PDB, WT·chain·resnum·mut in PDB numbering, and ddG, but **no pH, temperature or method**.

**Sign.** In the file, ddG < 0 = destabilising (mean −0.96), so it was flipped: `ddg = −ddg_orig`. Check after the flip: buried hydrophobic → small/polar gives mean +2.24, 94% > 0.

**Structures.**
- From the local wwPDB mirror (2A01 comes from `obsolete/`).
- Model 1, the mutated chain only, waters/H/ligands removed, first altloc, MSE→MET. Written to `data/structures/xtal/pdb/<PDB>_<chain>.pdb`.
- `assembly = asym_unit_model1_chain_only`.
- `near_hetatm` / `near_other_chain` (5 Å) are computed on the original entry: 10.0% and 10.2% of rows.

**Mapping.** Exact author numbering with the WT letter required to match: 669/669, 0 dropped.

**Columns.**
- `split = external`, `direction = direct`, `pair_id` = row id.
- `cluster` = homology family from `14_homology.py`.

**Homology.**
- 7/94 proteins (27.1% of rows) have a Megascale-train homologue (E ≤ 1e-3, ≥25% identity, ≥50% of the shorter sequence aligned); 5 of these are at ≥50% identity: 1O6X, 2HBB, 1DIV and 2WQG are 100% identical to Megascale domains and 3DV0_I is 98% identical to 1W4G. This is consistent with ThermoMPNN's `s669_vs_megascale_homologues.m8`.
- 7/94 proteins have a FireProt-trainval homologue.
- For a strict subset, filter `homolog_in_megascale_train == 0` (and `homolog_in_fireprot_trainval == 0`).

ddg: mean 0.96, sd 1.64, 44.5% > 1 kcal/mol.
